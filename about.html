About Spice & Ember

Where the ancient art of fire cooking meets modern culinary innovation

A Passion for Fire

Spice & Ember was born from a simple belief: that fire is the most powerful tool in a chef's arsenal. Our founder, Chef Marcus Flamewood, spent years traveling the world — from the asados of Argentina to the tandoor ovens of India — learning the ancient techniques of cooking over open flame.

In 2010, he returned with a vision: to create a restaurant where every dish tells a story of fire, spice, and craftsmanship. Today, Spice & Ember stands as a testament to that vision.

What We Stand For

Fire First

Every dish begins with flame. We believe fire unlocks flavors that no other cooking method can achieve.

Local & Seasonal

We source from farms and purveyors within 100 miles, ensuring peak freshness and supporting our community.

Craft Over Speed

Great food takes time. We honor the process — from dry-aging meats to slow-smoking over hardwoods.

Warm Hospitality

Every guest is family. We create an atmosphere where everyone feels welcome and well-cared for.
